The system

Humans set the intent. Agents do the work. Interfaces hold it together.

selfdrivenOS splits its technology into interfaces, each one a single boundary of the system. Every interface can be built, verified, and replaced on its own, and every action that crosses one traces back to a person.

Interface code

Eight interfaces, one organisational system.

Each interface is its own codebase in the selfdriven-tech organisation on GitHub, covering one boundary of selfdrivenOS.

Together they run from the person at the top to the infrastructure underneath — and every one can be adopted, audited, or swapped out on its own.

  • 01 · interface-humanHumanPeople-facing surfaces: sign-in, passkey wallets, and the conductor's view of the work.
  • 02 · interface-socialSocialThe social layer on the AT Protocol: portable identity, posts, and connections.
  • 03 · interface-ssiSSISelf-sovereign identity: KERI identifiers, witnessed key events, and ACDC credentials.
  • 04 · interface-aiAIIntelligence services and agents acting under scoped, revocable, delegated authority.
  • 05 · interface-entityEntityOrganisations, teams, and their structures — the entities that hold roles and act.
  • 06 · interface-attachmentAttachmentFiles, evidence, and media attached to entities and events, with their provenance.
  • 07 · interface-onchainOnChainOn-chain anchoring, tokens, and transactions, with Cardano as the base layer.
  • 08 · interface-infraInfraThe infrastructure the interfaces run on: hosting, deployment, and operations.
